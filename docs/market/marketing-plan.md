# Bakaya — Marketing Plan v1

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **For:** ETL SAPIENS / founders · **Status:** Full draft for review; all 13 sections pending user review. No section is represented as approved. Planning horizon begins after the hackathon; milestones are hypotheses and gates, not achieved results.

## 1. Executive summary

Bakaya should first prove that a CA can turn a client's disorganized receivables evidence into a reviewable packet with less preparation effort. The initial business is document preparation software for supported micro/small supplier cases. Recovery, legal entitlement, forum acceptance and time to payment are separate outcomes that Bakaya cannot promise.

**Bet 1: make the CA the repeat buyer.** Owners may face a large problem infrequently, while an accountant with several supplier clients may encounter it repeatedly. The accountant also understands the records and can judge whether the packet saves work. This is a plausible distribution hypothesis, not evidence that CAs will pay. The first research must establish monthly case frequency, current process, authority to upload client data and willingness to pay for preparation without representation.

**Bet 2: make verifiability the demonstration.** Show a traceable total, a payment difference left unresolved and a corrected schedule. A synthetic baseline/fix comparison is suitable hackathon proof if clearly labeled, but it does not establish real-world accuracy. One demonstrably reviewable packet can become a short demo, a sample download, a CA training exercise and a source-linked educational article. This is the main reusable marketing asset; it earns trust more directly than broad claims about AI.

**Bet 3: connect educational intent to a usable preparation workflow.** A single-invoice calculator and a document checklist can help prospects assess their situation. Each must explain its assumptions, date rules and eligibility limitations. Calculator visitors are not automatically customers. Measure whether qualified reviewers proceed to a sample packet or pilot, and avoid collecting sensitive evidence before the product is ready to handle it.

The next 90 days should produce a small body of customer evidence: permissioned workflow observations, five paid pilot packets if the value is real, measured preparation/review times, actual delivery/support costs, and a repeat-use signal from CAs. These are learning targets, not promised results. If most users need representation or cannot establish evidence, a professional-assisted model may be the better direction; if cases are rare, pay-per-packet may fit better than monthly plans.

Twelve months could plausibly establish a narrow CA-led business with one repeatable acquisition channel, reliable case boundaries and contribution-positive packaging. Fifty paying CA accounts is an explicit scenario, not a forecast. Its ₹2,49,950 MRR requires recurring willingness to pay, legal/product readiness and enough support capacity. Growth should be funded and expanded only as cohort evidence improves.

The 90-day priorities are: correct legal and pricing claims; ship a transparent synthetic demo; observe real preparation work with permission; deliver reviewed pilots; measure repeat usage and cost; publish a small set of useful resources; and decide whether the CA subscription is warranted. No ad budget or external outreach has been authorized by this document.

## 2. Strategic frame

**Positioning:** “Turn invoices, payments and delivery records into a claim-preparation packet your reviewer can check, with figures linked to their sources.” Category: evidence preparation for delayed-payment claims. This complements accounting software and professional advice. It does not establish a new legal entitlement.

**Primary ICP hypothesis:** an Indian CA/accounting firm serving micro/small manufacturing or service suppliers, with recurring overdue domestic B2B invoices, messy exports and someone accountable for review. The user may be a junior accountant; the buyer is the partner/owner; the client supplier must consent to data handling and approve their facts. Choose one reachable business cluster and language first, based on founder access rather than invented market share.

**Secondary ICP:** an owner with one supported, documented claim and willingness to self-file after review. **Potential channel:** a lawyer or professional filing service wanting preparation software. **Outside MVP:** medium enterprises, trade-only cases, disputed quality/acceptance, insolvency/limitation complexity, international/multi-currency claims and requests for representation. Outside MVP means human referral or unsupported case, not a definitive statement that no remedy exists.

The pain is potentially large but episodic for an owner; it could be large and frequent across a CA's client portfolio. The business-quality gate is therefore repeat case frequency. If a firm handles fewer than three suitable packets a quarter, the monthly bundle may be wasteful. If most preparation time is professional legal judgment rather than extraction/reconciliation, software savings may be limited. Establish those facts before adding sales capacity.

The business model hypothesis is free utility → paid packet → recurring CA usage. Its mechanism is reduced preparation/review time and clearer evidence. Value must be demonstrated without treating claim value or statutory interest as guaranteed recovered revenue. Source-linked numbers, explicit unresolved items and a dated rate table are candidate differentiators; competitors already advertise overlapping preparation, interest and filing services.

Voice: calm, specific and respectful of an owner's buyer relationships. Prefer “prepare,” “review,” “source,” “estimate,” “assumption” and “supported case.” Avoid “guaranteed recovery,” “only AI solution,” “get paid in 90 days,” “government approved” and blanket tax threats. Synthetic stories must say synthetic. Use measured results with dataset size/version, and dated statutory sources with legal review status. Never invent endorsements, user counts or customer quotes.

Behavior principles to test: reduce activation energy with a sample; reduce status-quo friction by accepting existing exports; use progressive disclosure so the first task is understandable; make unresolved work visible without coercive urgency. The default must preserve user control: no automatic sending, no preselected subscription, no hidden renewal. Social proof means permissioned genuine testimony only. Do not apply decoys, fear or artificial scarcity to a financially stressed supplier.

## 4. Acquisition

**Now: founder-led learning through trusted contacts.** After explicit authorization, invite known CAs/owners to explain the last case they prepared. Offer a synthetic packet demonstration and a checklist. Track qualified conversations, accepted observation sessions and pilot interest. A helpful reply is not paid demand. Stop or change segment if ten qualified discussions reveal neither frequent cases nor a preparation problem.

**Now: sample packet and checklist.** Create one public synthetic example with evidence links, eligibility caveats and unresolved items; pair it with a short “documents to gather” guide. Owner: builder+reviewer. Leading indicator: qualified reviewer can locate three supporting documents without assistance. Reuse the asset for the demo, sales walkthrough and founder content.

**Q1: narrow educational search.** Draft pages on acceptance vs invoice date, why a payment difference may be TDS, and what to prepare before using ODR. Date and source every legal claim; let the reviewer approve before publication. Unknown query volumes mean this is an intent hypothesis, not an SEO forecast. Track qualified sample requests rather than pageviews alone. Review content when law/rates/portal rules change.

**Q1: one founder channel.** LinkedIn is the proposed CA/business learning channel; X can support hackathon visibility. Two useful posts/week is a capacity ceiling, not a requirement. Post the specific problem, a synthetic source-link example and measured evaluation limitations. No automated outreach or publication is included here. Judge attention and buyer validation are different funnels.

**Q2: accountant/association demonstrations.** Test one small webinar or office session with an expert, based on permissioned interest. Measure attended qualified firms → completed evaluation → paid pilot. Do not buy a large conference sponsorship before this conversion path is credible.

**Deferred:** Google Search test only after five paid pilots, a working conversion page, positive contribution and approval of a bounded budget. LinkedIn paid, broad social ads, influencers, mass cold outreach and city-by-city SEO are low priority for this stage. No search-volume or ad-CAC data supports them today.

90-day deliverables: one reusable sample, three reviewed learning pages, 10–15 qualitative conversations if authorized, and one cohort review. Twelve-month direction: extend only the channel that supplies suitable cases profitably; stage one additional channel while the first remains productive. Skills: competitors, content-strategy, seo-audit, social, customer-research. Tools: local evidence register now; consented CRM and analytics later, connection unverified.

## 5. Activation

Define activation as **a qualified reviewer finishes one supported packet, resolves or explicitly classifies every blocker, and can find the evidence for selected figures**. Upload or signup alone is not value.

Proposed flow: try sample → understand supported scope → identify supplier/buyer → add records → review extraction and allocation → resolve legal/date uncertainties → review schedule → approve export. Offer a sample without asking for real bank statements. Give a precise checklist and an explanation for every required field. Preserve the existing accounting workflow rather than demanding a migration.

Instrument pseudonymous steps without raw invoice/bank data in analytics: sample_started, scope_checked, case_started, evidence_added, review_started, blocker_opened, blocker_resolved, review_approved, packet_exported. Record abandonment reason, reviewer time and staff help. Never count an unresolved fallback as a ready statutory packet just to improve funnel conversion.

First five permissioned usability sessions should test: can the reviewer explain the date basis, distinguish inferred TDS from verified TDS, find a source, spot a stale rate and understand that approval is not filing? Use the observations to reduce confusing fields. With a small cohort, prefer observed task failures over underpowered A/B tests.

90-day learning targets: at least 4 of 5 reviewers complete the synthetic task; no critical unsupported figure; median reviewer time reported with range. These are proposed gates, not achieved metrics. Twelve months: stable imports and role separation only when repeated demand justifies them. Skills: onboarding, signup, copywriting, marketing-psychology. Current tools: local synthetic demo; analytics and identity integrations not assumed live.

## 6. Retention

Owners with a single case should be able to complete the job and leave. Retention should come from new useful work, not keeping a financial record hostage. For CAs, measure a second supported client/case within 60 days and paid account retention with usage; distinguish a month with no cases from product dissatisfaction.

Proposed first-month service: permissioned onboarding help; a check after the first packet to identify corrections; a second-case walkthrough if relevant; a monthly usage summary only by consent. Include download/deletion and clear cancellation terms. Avoid urging users to escalate a dispute as an engagement tactic.

Support questions should become reviewed help content only after removing identifying evidence. Track corrections per packet, support minutes, repeated failure reasons, deletion requests and refund reasons. Legal/portal/rate changes require targeted notices to affected users only through authorized channels; a marketing newsletter is not a substitute for correcting an affected output.

90 days: document each pilot's return/reason-not-to-return; test one revised checklist. Twelve months: cohort retention by CA segment and case frequency, bounded support and a versioned change process. Skills: emails, churn-prevention, customer-research. No email automation is currently verified.

## 7. Referral

Ask for introductions only after a user has completed a valuable reviewed packet. A simple permissioned introduction to another accountant is preferable to rewards before the model is understood. Count referred qualified firms and their paid activation, not invite counts.

The PRD's “prepared with Bakaya” demand-letter loop is speculative. A demand letter is sensitive business communication, so branding should be optional and removable, with supplier approval. Do not turn legal correspondence into forced advertising. Avoid automatically contacting the buyer's finance team. CA professional independence and referral-compensation rules need review before incentives.

90 days: learn whether satisfied pilots will make a voluntary introduction; target three introductions only as a research goal. Q2+: a clear partner page and onboarding guide if there is repeated interest. Twelve months: measure partner concentration, attribution and actual support cost. Skills: referrals, sales-enablement; tracking can begin in a local consented log.

## 13. Measurement, RACI and open decisions

**Proposed north-star:** monthly paid packets that a reviewer approves after evidence checks, accompanied by critical-correction rate and contribution margin. Count it once per buyer/version-defined job; retries do not inflate value. This measures delivered preparation, not money recovered. Track later recovery only when a customer supplies evidence and consents; never imply causal credit automatically.

| Stage | Leading measures | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
|Acquisition|Qualified conversations, sample evaluations, pilot requests by source|No scraped contact blast; include founder time in CAC|
|Activation|Review completion, time, source finding, unresolved blockers|Critical errors/unsupported values; no approval-rate gaming|
|Retention|Second case within 60 days, paid CA cohorts, reason for nonuse|Deletion/cancellation ease; no forced subscriptions|
|Referral|Consented introductions→qualified firms→paid use|No confidential case disclosure|
|Revenue|Cash paid, refunds, contribution, full utilization margin, CAC payback|Separate one-off and subscription economics|

Weekly: founder+builder review failures, learning and time/cost. Monthly: founder+reviewer assess legal/source changes and cohort evidence. Quarterly: reset ICP, packages, budget and deferred channels; no automatic spend increase.

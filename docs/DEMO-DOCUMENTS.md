# Demo documents

Use the built-in synthetic claim to demonstrate the complete reconciliation, review, approval and packet workflow. All supplier, buyer, invoice, payment, TDS and rate records are fictional. Original supporting text exports and structured input are bundled locally in artifacts/demo-inputs.zip.

For an internet-sourced OCR reference, use the unmodified [CORD receipt sample](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clovaai/donut/master/misc/sample_image_cord_test_receipt_00004.png). Attribution: CORD by Park et al./NAVER CLOVA, [dataset and CC BY 4.0 license](https://github.com/clovaai/cord). Source SHA-256: 8f3eee7068c96e86cdb2e4b5c53085cb5e1439462edd55c373548cb1962801ad.

This is a public Indonesian receipt, not an Indian receivables claim. Tested local OCR recovered zero of five expected monetary amounts. Demonstrate that it stays in review without fabricated facts; keep it in a separate case from the successful synthetic packet.

The public shared account exposes its records to every visitor. Never upload private financial documents. Internet availability alone does not establish permission, ownership or legal validity. The source reference establishes technical test provenance, not professional validation.

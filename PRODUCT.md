# Bakaya

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of Indian micro and small manufacturing or service businesses prepare delayed-payment claims. Chartered accountants are a proposed channel in the supplied PRD; no CA access or completed CA validation is claimed.

## Product Purpose

Assemble supplier records into a reviewable claim packet for self-filing. Success means supported payment allocations, dates, eligibility checks, deterministic interest, and explicit human approval before a local download.

## Positioning

Every amount and decision can be followed to uploaded evidence. Ambiguous payments and unreadable documents remain unresolved until reviewed.

## Operating Context

One supplier, one buyer, INR records, and local document preparation. Supported uploads include PDF, CSV, XLSX, text exports, and images. Scans without OCR remain visibly unreadable; structured records and optional model proposals require supported provenance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/TypeScript frontend and FastAPI backend. Monthly compound interest is computed with Decimal code using the backend's disclosed convention. Eligibility is a conservative preparation screen, not a legal conclusion. No portal filing or buyer communication occurs automatically. Source fingerprints identify stored bytes, not authenticity. The product must preserve unresolved blockers, approval invalidation after changes, audit entries, and original source access.

## Brand Commitments

The user approved a ledger-first main workspace (proposal A), an evidence-first review experience (proposal B), and refinement of the established green and ivory identity. Use plain language, readable financial records, and synthetic examples clearly labeled as such.

## Evidence on Hand

Supplied PRD: docs/original-prd.md. Synthetic fixtures and evaluation are implemented locally. Approved UI proposals: workspace-A.png and workspace-B.png in the user's Bakaya proposal folder. Proposal values are illustrative composition references; the implemented UI displays API values only. No real customer outcomes or CA approval are available.

## Product Principles

- Show the records before the totals.
- Keep evidence beside the decision it supports.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of filling gaps.
- Record human approval before exporting.
- Distinguish measured synthetic evaluation from real-world validation.

## Open Decisions

Real customer validation, verified RBI rate histories, final ODR field requirements, production deployment, and any real filing require separate verification.
